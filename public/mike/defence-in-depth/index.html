Defence in depth: an interactive guide

Defence in depth is a layered approach to information security. It assumes that no single control is sufficient, so independent measures are arranged so that an attacker must defeat several of them in turn. Select any layer to read further detail.

The monitoring and response element observes all seven layers and feeds findings back into each of them.

Layers are ordered from the outermost control at the top to the innermost asset at the bottom. The currently selected layer is indicated by a thicker border, an inset bar, and a selected marker, in addition to its colour.
