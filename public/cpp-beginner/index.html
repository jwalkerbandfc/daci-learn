      Keyboard and touch tips
      
          
        
        • Select a block on the canvas, then tap a palette block: it goes inside or under the selected one. "Show me where" selects the right place for you.
          • 
        
        • Shapes only fit where the code makes sense. A tag (data type) only fits a tag-shaped gap.
          • 
        
        • With a block selected: ↑ and ↓ move it, Delete removes it, Ctrl+Z undoes.
          • 
        
        • To delete by dragging, drop a block on the red bar at the bottom of the canvas, or anywhere over the palette.
          • 
        
        • A red ! marks something to fix. Hover over the block to see what.
